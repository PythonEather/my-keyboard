New: a **built-in 3D engine** for mods.

A `gl` layer can now have `scene: { ... }`: load `.obj` and `.glb` / `.gltf` models from a `.zip` mod (or use the built-in cube, plane, sphere, cylinder and quad), set a camera, up to 8 point and sun lights, fog and materials, and the keyboard draws them lit, with depth. The objects share the depth buffer with the layer's own shader, so a raymarcher or raycaster background can hide and show 3D objects. `kb.scene(id, patch)` moves the camera and objects while the mod runs. Works in JavaScript and Lua mods. Example: `gl_scene.js`, docs: the **3D scenes** section of `PLUGINS.md`.

Includes the 11.2.19 fix for Lua mods.
